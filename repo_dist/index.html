✨ Package Highlights

⚠️ Hardware Note: Tested & verified on iPhone 4s (iOS 9.3.6).

  • Lock-Free Audio Engine: SPSC Ring Buffer with zero real-time audio thread locks or allocations.
  • Multi-Format Decoding: Native FLAC, Opus, and uncompressed PCM streaming.
  • Sub-Millisecond Clock Sync: Kalman-filtered hardware timebase synchronization.
  • Universal iOS Compatibility: Optimized for iOS 9.3.6 (iPhone 4s) with full backwards compatibility to iOS 3.0.
  • System Integration: Native lockscreen, Control Center, and AirPlay metadata.
